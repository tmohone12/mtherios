# MythEngine Phase 6: local Research slice

Date: 2026-09-22; updated 2026-09-23. Status: **Phase 6 in progress** in the isolated `codex/mythengine-overhaul` working copy. The provider-backed cited synthesis and full qualification gate remain open.

The GM tool runner now exposes `research` under the saved `gmResearch` switch. Its local extractive helper reuses `memory.retrieve` and `world.search` with semantic vectors explicitly off, searches public authored files, follows a numeric transcript hit with `campaign.entriesAround`, and returns at most three short, exact source quotations. Before returning a citation, it checks that the quoted text is an exact substring of gathered evidence and verifies its current game/project scope and revision. Player-visible, committed corrections linked to gathered entries are rechecked against current canon before use; transcript entries they explicitly supersede are excluded. The shared narrative-event filter ignores pending player correction requests and transcript-deletion control events, and player actions cannot become factual answer statements. Missing evidence returns `insufficient_evidence`. A 7-second deadline or local failure returns `unavailable` so ordinary `read`, `search`, and `list` tools remain usable. The read-only preview honors the same off switch. The existing Active Recall path now preserves numeric `position`, `beforePosition`, `radius`, and typed campaign page arguments. A completed GM Research tool call suppresses a duplicate Active Recall planner pass; a saved Research-off value also disables that investigator.

This safe local mode includes player-visible transcript and correction evidence, explicitly public canonical records, and explicitly public unscoped authored files. It does not synthesize a new factual sentence or send gathered Research excerpts to a provider. GM-only or secret records are intentionally outside this tool's answer set. The prior Active Recall service remains a separate configured feature when enabled; this slice does not expand its provider payload.
